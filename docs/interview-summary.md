# Interview summary: forming teams for group coursework

**Project:** SETaP Team Formation Manager, Group 2C

We held two semi-structured, audio-recorded interviews with lecturers who run group coursework. Names are removed. Times such as (05:47) are positions in the recording. Figures given by lecturers are their own estimates, not measured data.

## Interview 1

- **Random allocation** takes no staff time, so staff like it (03:35). **Student choice** may give students more ownership, but takes longer and is hit-or-miss (00:54, 03:54).
- **How often groups go wrong:** up to about 20% with either method, with about 10–15% breaking down completely. It depends on the year group (01:44).
- **No single measure is enough.** Attendance is a good benchmark but says less when it is enforced (05:47, 06:27). Prior grades are not enough on their own, and similar ability does not guarantee that students work well together (04:20). Combine measures (07:26).
- **Student questionnaire:** past group work, whether groups were random or chosen, communication, resolving problems, leadership, initiative, rough attendance and past breakdowns. Expect some answers to be untrue (11:05, 11:39).
- **Anonymous categories:** group students into types (A, B, C) using attributes such as communication, initiative, grades and attendance (18:59).
- **Mixed groups preferred:** spread the categories so that weaker students are not all placed together (13:16, 15:31).
- **Staff control:** staff choose random, ability-based, mixed or hybrid allocation, and can move students by hand afterwards (18:08, 20:03–21:55).
- **Privacy:** students should not see category data, because it could distort the groups (20:32).
- **Follow-up:** collect student feedback on the groups and use it to adjust the method (21:55). Students who cannot work together should be handled by hand for now (24:34).

## Interview 2

- **Students choose their own groups** of 2 to 5, within the same timetabled session. Grades are never used, because it is unclear what is fair (00:48–01:38, 02:44).
- **Timing:** groups form at the start of the second term, over two weeks, once students know each other (02:04–02:53).
- **Non-attenders are grouped together,** so engaged students are not disadvantaged. A late student joins a group only if it agrees. At least 75% of these groups pass, at the low end (02:59, 18:14–20:57).
- **Outcome:** choosing works about 90% of the time, helped by a group contract (03:20–03:50).
- **Group contract:** meeting time and place, shared workspace, tasks with deadlines, a communication channel and any extra terms. Agreed within 7 days and worth 5% of the coursework (04:05–06:24, 10:57).
- **Contribution** is judged yes or no against the contract, on evidence. If a member met the agreed terms, a complaint about them does not stand (09:02–10:04, 28:06).
- **Changing group:** needs a real reason and both groups' agreement, with no group above 5. Staff are informed. Changes happen early, rarely midway and never at the end (06:36, 32:14–34:13).
- **Staff step in** only in extreme cases, one or two a year among 300–400 students (07:06–08:46).
- **If groups had to be formed in week 1** (never tried): a bonding activity and a role activity, then random allocation aiming for a mix of roles, giving little or no weight to first-year grades (22:04–24:14).
- **Advice for the system:** students rate team roles green, amber or red; attendance is the strongest positive sign of engagement; engagement and personality matter more than grades; groups manage themselves (24:14–24:59, 31:01–34:13).

## Where the interviews agree and differ

| Topic | Interview 1 | Interview 2 | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attendance | Useful, but weaker when enforced | Strongest sign of engagement | Mostly agree |
| Mixing students | Spread student types across groups | Mix roles if groups must be allocated | Agree on mixing |
| Asking students | Questionnaire on past group work and skills | Students rate their own roles | Agree |
| Who forms groups | Staff choose the method and allocate | Students choose within staff rules | Differ |
| Grades | Useful when combined with other measures | Never used when students choose | Differ |
| Weaker or absent students | Do not place weaker students together | Group non-attenders together | Differ |
| Changing groups | Staff can move students | Both groups agree; staff informed | Differ |
| Staff involvement | Staff keep control | Staff step in only in extreme cases | Differ |
| Category visibility | Hidden from students | Not raised | No comparison |
